## Statistical Measures Used

**1. Mean:** Calculates the arithmetic average of the values.

**2. Median:** Identifies the middle value and is less affected by extreme values than the mean.

**3. Mode:** Identifies the most frequently occurring value.

**4. Standard Deviation:** Measures how widely values vary around the mean.

**5. Percentiles:** Show the distribution of values at specified points, including the 25th, 50th, and 75th percentiles.

**6. Interquartile Range (IQR):** Measures the spread of the middle 50% of observations using Q3 − Q1.

**7. Skewness:** Indicates the asymmetry of a distribution. Positive skewness indicates a longer right tail, while negative skewness indicates a longer left tail.
